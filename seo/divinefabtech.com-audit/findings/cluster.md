## Keywords and notes per cluster

**Local tarpaulin manufacturer - Ahmedabad / Gujarat / Rupal-Bavla**: multilayer tarpaulin manufacturer in ahmedabad, tarpaulin manufacturer in gujarat, tadpatri manufacturer ahmedabad, multilayer tarpaulin manufacturer rupal bavla.

Single-manufacturer pages hold 38% of slots, so the page type is winnable, but the majority result is a multi-supplier list showing prices and ratings. Winning manufacturer pages are product pages with the city in the title (auitarp.com #2, shreebalahanumanpolycoat.com #4) plus Frank Industries' city blog post. Divine's landing page is not in any of these top 10s; for the Rupal-Bavla query Google picks the blog guide (#4) and the old vercel.app home (#10) instead, and NK Polycross holds #1/#3/#5 via IndiaMART, its About page and TradeIndia. URL overlap: multilayer tarpaulin manufacturer in ahmedabad x tarpaulin manufacturer in gujarat = 5; multilayer tarpaulin manufacturer in ahmedabad x tadpatri manufacturer ahmedabad = 3; multilayer tarpaulin manufacturer in ahmedabad x multilayer tarpaulin manufacturer rupal bavla = 3; tarpaulin manufacturer in gujarat x tadpatri manufacturer ahmedabad = 3; tarpaulin manufacturer in gujarat x multilayer tarpaulin manufacturer rupal bavla = 1; tadpatri manufacturer ahmedabad x multilayer tarpaulin manufacturer rupal bavla = 0.

**Multilayer tarpaulin - national product/category intent**: multilayer tarpaulin manufacturer in india.

6 of 10 results are manufacturer product/category pages (Amrut SEALTARP, Supreme XF, Essen, PMA, NK Polycross, Shree Bala Hanuman). Divine's product page is the right type but has about 240 words against NK Polycross' long spec page with GSM-by-GSM test tables. Only 1 shared URL with the Ahmedabad cluster and 1 with 'tarpaulin manufacturer in india', so it needs its own page; /products/tarpaulins (H1 'Multilayer Tarpaulin') fits better than the landing page.

**Tarpaulin manufacturer India - head term**: tarpaulin manufacturer in india.

Weak consensus: manufacturer pages 50%, but 3 slots go to listicles/social (Instagram 'Top 10', ROC Tarp 'Top 7', a Facebook video) and #1 is IndiaMART. Winners are big brands with review stars (Comsyn 5.0, 47 reviews; Shalimar 4.8). Page type fits; the gap is authority. Keep this slug for the head term and broaden the title beyond 'multilayer'.

**LDPE tarpaulin**: ldpe tarpaulin manufacturer.

4 of 10 are IndiaMART-style catalogue pages titled 'LDPE Tarpaulin ...'. Divine makes LDPE/LLDPE/mLDPE multilayer tarpaulin (never HDPE) but 'LDPE' is not in the product page title or H1. Interlinks with the Ahmedabad cluster (2 shared URLs: Frank's LDPE Ahmedabad post, Kashyap Unitex). A dedicated LDPE section or page is the cheapest gap to close.

**Tarpaulin by GSM - 90 GSM**: 90 gsm tarpaulin.

8 of 10 results are single products with a visible price (5 ecommerce, 3 marketplace product listings: Nakoda Tarp Ahmedabad Rs150/kg, Dunlop Rs165/kg, TradeIndia Rs220). Divine's tarpaulin page shows no price and has no Product/Offer schema (only /products/poncho has it). The one content slot is Welpack's 'Is 90 GSM tarpaulin good?' (#5), which /blog/tarpaulin-gsm-guide can contest.

**Tarpaulin price per kg**: tarpaulin price per kg.

Every useful result shows a price (IndiaMART Rs145-172/kg, Cooverji size price list, Shree Kushal Rs115/kg, Pooja Enterprises' 'Tarpaulin Price in India' guide updated 3 days before capture). No Divine page states any tarpaulin price; the nearest is /blog/tarpaulin-gsm-guide. Needs a new price-explainer page (how Rs/kg is set by GSM and size, worked example, current band if the business agrees).
