What to do next, most important first

  1. Get your other pages into Google. In Search Console, open Sitemaps and submit sitemap.xml, then open URL Inspection for each product and search page and press Request indexing. Your search pages were written for exactly these keywords, but Google can only rank a page it has indexed.
  2. List on IndiaMART and tidy your Justdial listing. IndiaMART is first for most product searches here, and many buyers search inside it directly. Your Justdial listing appears as “Divine Fab Tech” in Adroda: make its name, address and phone match your website.
  3. Set up a Google Business Profile for the factory if you don't have one, so you can appear in map results for searches like “tarpaulin manufacturer near me”. Those map results are not counted on this page.
  4. Move to your own domain when you can. A .vercel.app address is harder for buyers and Google to trust than a domain of your own.

How this was checked

  • Each keyword was searched once on 26 Oct 2026 with the location set to India, and the first 30 results were read. The search was not personalised and leaves out map results, so a buyer's own Google can differ by a few places.
  • Average position, impressions and clicks are page-level Search Console figures, read through Google Analytics. Google Analytics can't list the exact phrases people searched; Search Console's Performance report can.
  • The site: check used the same web search index, which can lag behind Google. Search Console agrees that only the home page is showing.
