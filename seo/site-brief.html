Questions for you, round 1

The skill asks two or three at a time, starting with the ones that change later steps the most. Reply in chat, in any order, short answers are fine.

  1. Should searches target India, in English? GA agrees: 35 of 50 sessions are from India, mostly Ahmedabad. Do you also want full Hindi or Gujarati pages, or keep the local words inside English pages as now?
